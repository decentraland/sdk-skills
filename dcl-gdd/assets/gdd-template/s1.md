## 1. Player Promise

**One-line promise** *— maximum 25 words.*

> `You are [role or situation]. You [main verb] to [goal], while [distinctive tension or twist].`

*The exact sentence shape is optional; those four pieces are not. A stranger should be able to repeat it after hearing it once. Example: "You are a night-shift courier. You deliver packages across a sleeping city, while every delivery wakes something up." The filled example (`gdd-example.md`) shows another one.*

## Before you submit — self-check, delete before submitting

*The criteria and thresholds below are the program's — you do not invent a measurement framework, and the [dashboard](https://decentraland.zone/creators-data/dashboard.html) already tracks return for you. Do not plan around custom in-game tracking.*

| Criterion | Decision rule |
|---|---|
| **Immediate comprehension** | The 5/10 rule (Section 2): once playable, at least 80% perform the first useful action by 5 seconds and state the immediate goal by 10 seconds without help. |
| **Voluntary continuation** | Without prompting, players choose another loop or round, invite, regroup, or ask when they can return (Sections 2, 4). |
| **Progression and return** | Progress changes later play (Section 4); the v1 floor is >10% first-week return (dashboard D7). |
| **Intentional social play** | A voluntary action changes another player's play, options, reward, status, or relationship; joining or leaving does not break play (Section 5). |
| **Social continuity** | Returning players recognize people, roles, relationships, routines, reputation, or shared history; at least one changes a later session (Section 5). |
| **Visible, shareable play** | Nearby users can understand the activity, and play creates a specific moment worth sharing (Sections 5, 7). |
| **Discoverability Bar** | Packaging, spawn, and first-minute play promise the same experience, with no unresolved IP or Content Policy issue (Sections 1–2, 7). |

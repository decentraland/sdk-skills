# Arrange research for the current design question

Read when preparing or executing agreed research, including a saved assignment for later.
The interview uses topic guides to decide what to offer; this guide organises the work.

**Facts are yours, decisions are theirs.** Research benchmarks, comparables and platform
capabilities when needed for the decision; connect findings to this game's choices.
Once research is agreed, delegate it to background subagents when available and permitted.
**For deep research, prefer a strong reasoning model with high effort**, within the owner's
model preferences and budget. Set model and effort through supported agent launch controls;
prompt wording alone does not configure them. If selection is unavailable, use the available
configuration and state the limitation; report only settings you can verify. Keep brief fact
checks in the current interview configuration.
Use [research delivery](research-delivery.md) to divide work, save findings during
the search and leave resources for synthesis. Pass that guide and the relevant topic reference
below to each worker, with the current design context, question and agreed breadth/budget;
include their instructions if the worker cannot read the files.
For DCL dependencies, first use [platform knowledge](platform-fit.md#reuse-the-platform-answer-before-researching-it-again)
and pass applicable settled facts as inputs, their source/scope and the remaining gap to each worker
and its descendants. Reuse documented feasibility; do not confuse an untested game feel with
an unknown platform capability or send every worker on the same platform survey.
Continue the interview on topics that do not depend on the findings, keeping dependent
conclusions pending. Without delegation, use the same delivery guidance in a sequential pass.
For focused research into games, developer accounts or implementations, read
[prior art](prior-art.md). For
budget or affordability claims, first apply [funding and scope](funding-and-scope.md).
Before player-dependent design decisions, use [player mode](platform-fit.md#player-mode).
When shaping the play space, suggesting platform possibilities or estimating implementation
effort, use [platform-fit.md](platform-fit.md), including its Worlds-first default.

Use the [shared evidence rules](../SKILL.md#hard-rules) in every result and handoff.
